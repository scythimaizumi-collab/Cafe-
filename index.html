Campus Café

Good coffee, honest food, and the best study tables on campus — run by students, for students.

See the menu

A living room between lectures

Tucked into the Student Union Building, Campus Café is run by a rotating crew of student baristas who believe a great flat white can rescue a terrible timetable.

We opened in 2023 with one secondhand espresso machine and a borrowed bookshelf. Two years later we serve over 300 cups a day, host weekly open mics, and still keep a "pay it forward" board where anyone can buy a coffee for a stranger. Every pastry is baked on campus by the culinary club, and our beans are roasted two streets away.
